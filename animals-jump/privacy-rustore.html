  • Игра ничего не собирает и никуда не передаёт. В ней нет рекламы, покупок, аналитики, регистрации и сторонних SDK, которые собирают данные. Игра не выходит в интернет.

  • Игра подходит для всех возрастов; данных детей мы не собираем.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

Animals Jump (version without ads and purchases; the "Game"). Developer: Dottyl ([developer's full name]). Contact: [e-mail].

  • The Game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. It has no ads, no purchases, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The Game does not use the internet.
  • Progress (best scores, coins, upgrades, tasks, settings) is stored in the Game's private folder on your device and is deleted together with the Game (or when you clear its data in Android settings). This data is never sent to us, and we store nothing on any server.
  • If Android backup is turned on, the system may save your progress in your backup. This backup is managed by Android itself and we have no access to it.
  • The only permission the Game uses is vibration.
  • The Game is suitable for all ages; we do not collect data from children.
  • We may update this policy; the current version is always at this link.
